About Sara Larson

Sara Larson is a seasoned HR professional with expertise in public sector and small business human resources, employee relations, compensation, and regulatory compliance. With a master's degree in management and leadership and years of hands-on leadership in North Carolina county government, Sara brings a grounded, practical perspective to every engagement.

At Larson HR Consulting, Sara partners with local governments, nonprofits, and small organizations to build HR systems that are fair, sound, and built to last — without the overhead of a large consulting firm. Whether you need a compensation study, policy review, or ongoing HR guidance, you get direct access to someone who has done the work.

Services

Compensation Studies

Market-based pay analysis, salary schedule development, and classification reviews grounded in regional data and NC statutory requirements.

Policy Development

Clear, compliant personnel policies and employee handbooks tailored to your organization's size, structure, and governing framework.

Compliance & Risk

Guidance on federal and NC employment law, FLSA compliance, FMLA/ADA administration, and workplace investigation support.

Employee Relations

Grievance process design, disciplinary review, performance management frameworks, and coaching for supervisors and HR teams.

HR Audits

Comprehensive reviews of your HR practices, documentation, and processes to identify gaps and prioritize improvements.

Interim HR Support

Fractional HR leadership for organizations between hires or navigating a busy season — providing hands-on support without the cost of a full-time position.
